================================================================================
TODO LIST - QUICK START
================================================================================
A small to-do app for the Mac. Needs macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, on Apple
silicon or Intel. Takes about two minutes to set up.


1. INSTALL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Drag "Todo List.app" into your Applications folder.

Run it from Applications, not from Downloads. The app opens itself at login,
and macOS remembers where it was when that was switched on.


2. OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The app is free and isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch.
You only have to do this once.

macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later:
  1. Double-click Todo List. macOS says it "could not verify" the app.
     Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
     Next to "Todo List was blocked", click Open Anyway.
  3. Enter your password, then click Open Anyway again.

macOS 13 or 14:
  Control-click (or right-click) Todo List in Applications, choose Open, then
  click Open in the dialog.

If neither works, paste this into Terminal and press Return, then open the app
normally:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Todo List.app"


3. THE TWO PROMPTS YOU'LL SEE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "Todo List would like to access files in your Documents folder."
  Click Allow (or OK). Your tasks are saved there. If you say no, the app shows
  a banner and saves nothing until you allow it.

- A notification that a login item was added. That's this app, so the
  shortcut below works as soon as you log in. To turn it off, choose
  Todo List > Open at Login from the menu bar.


4. USING IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Control-Option-Space (⌃⌥Space) shows the window from any app. Press it
again to hide it.

Closing the window doesn't quit the app, so the shortcut keeps working. Use
Cmd-Q to quit. Clicking the Dock icon also brings the window back.

The tabs at the top:
  Short Term   Readings, Assignments, Emails, and Other, on one screen
  Long Term    one plain list
  Due          every task that has a due date, soonest first

The basics:
  - Type in a section's field and press Return to add a task.
  - Click the box to finish a task.
  - Hover over a task for the star, the calendar (due date), the pie
    (part done, in quarters), and the x (delete).
  - Double-click a task to rename it.
  - Cmd-Z undoes anything, including a delete.
  - Cmd-1 to Cmd-4 jump to a section's field, and Cmd-L to the long-term one.

The full list of features and shortcuts is in the README:
https://github.com/ruben-hayrapetyan/ToDo


5. YOUR DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything is in one file, saved the moment you change anything:

    Documents/Todo List/todos.json

To move your tasks to another Mac, copy that file. A copy from the start of
each day is kept in Documents/Todo List/saves.


6. IF SOMETHING'S WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Control-Option-Space is already in use", or the shortcut does nothing:
  Another app has that shortcut. Click the Dock icon to open the window
  instead.

The shortcut switches your keyboard language:
  macOS uses the same keys for "Select next source in Input menu". Turn that
  off in System Settings > Keyboard > Keyboard Shortcuts > Input Sources.

A banner says the task file can't be opened:
  Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Files and Folders, switch on
  Documents Folder under Todo List, then quit and reopen the app.


7. UPDATING AND REMOVING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Update: quit with Cmd-Q and replace the app in Applications with the new one.
Your tasks stay put.

Remove: turn off Todo List > Open at Login, quit with Cmd-Q, and drag the app
to the Trash. Your tasks stay in Documents/Todo List until you delete that
folder.
